Most of these examples were built by AI from a single prompt. The animation, the interaction, the whole experience.

Imagine a chat that belongs on your website. Something that would engage your visitors to a conversation you and them both would learn from. Give AI your idea, this repo, and the custom chat UI guide. Stand Chat handles the backend. See an original prompt.
