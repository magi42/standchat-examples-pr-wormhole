How it works

A conversation across spacetime

Wormhole turns a Stand conversation into a transmission from a starship. Your message leaves the console one letter at a time, spirals through a gravitational lens, and reaches a responder on the other side. Their reply travels back into the same readable transcript.

Try it

  1. Watch the ten-second approach, or choose Enter the bridge to skip it.
  2. Type a message and press Enter. Watch the letters spiral into the wormhole, then follow the reply back to the console. Shift + Enter adds a new line.
  3. Use Motion to pause the effects, Sound for the optional synthesized audio, and Intro to replay the approach. Reduced-motion preferences skip the intro and letter flights automatically.

The conversation is Stand

stand-client.js adapts the Visitor API client used by the other custom chat examples. It discovers a responder using the shared demo Site ID, creates a conversation on your first message, sends over HTTP, and receives replies over a WebSocket. The console identifies AI Stand-ins and human team members, restores conversations after reloads, and offers recovery when a connection fails.

const client = new StandClient({
  siteId: 'demo',
  storage,
  analyticsId: 'wormhole-bridge',
  greeting,
  prompt: 'You are Horizon, a friendly AI communications officer…',
});

client.subscribe(state => {
  // Render the canonical transcript; animate newly received messages.
});
client.mount();

// A session is created only when the visitor sends a message.
client.setDraft(input.value);
await client.send();

The flight is presentation

scene.js draws a procedural starfield and spherical gravitational lens with WebGL. Canvas 2D moves the message glyphs along curved paths. Incoming replies wait for the outgoing flight, then settle into ordinary selectable HTML. Long messages use a short visual excerpt while their full text stays in the transcript. Pausing motion reveals pending replies immediately; a CSS lens remains visible when WebGL is unavailable.

The exterior approach is an original SVG spacecraft animated with CSS, not a video download. The ship, crew silhouettes, and telemetry are fictional. Audio starts only when enabled and pauses when the page is hidden.

Copy this example

npx degit standchat/examples/wormhole my-wormhole

Serve the folder over HTTP, then replace siteId: 'demo' in app.js and the Stand installation snippet in index.html with your own Site ID. The shared /_shared/ files only provide the gallery navigation, explanation styles, and site analytics; the bridge and chat work without them.
